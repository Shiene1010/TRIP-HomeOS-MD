[Topic 1] 07:45 AM — 우유 낙하 & 등원 준비 (Spill & Departure)

공통 상황: 도도가 식탁에 우유를 쏟고 신발을 신지 않은 상태
• 👩 엄마표 (Mom-mode / G1~3 맞벌이 기본)
	• T: "The milk cup slipped near Dodo."
	• R: 1. Dodo, wipe the milk off with the blue towel. 2. Put sneakers on by the front door.
	• IP: "Is the kitchen table dry? (Yes/No)"
• 👨 아빠표 (Dad-mode / 결과·시간 중심)
	• T: "The bus leaves in 5 minutes."
	• R: Wipe spill, wear shoes, stand by the front door.
	• IP: "Are shoes on? (Yes)"
• 🧔 삼촌표 (Uncle-mode / 미션 협상형)
	• T: "Table is wet."
	• R: Finish wiping first, then grab your backpack, Dodo.
	• IP: "Can we leave before wiping? (No)"
• 👵 할머니표 (Grandma-mode / 느린 시각·안전)
	• T: "The floor near Dodo is wet and cold."
	• R: Step back. Use the blue towel, Dodo.
	• IP: "Did you step back safely? (Yes)"
• 👩‍🏫 선생님표 (Teacher-mode / 규제 STE 표준)
	• T: "Spill detected at station."
	• R: 1. Apply towel to surface. 2. Place footwear in position.
	• IP: "Is surface clear? (Yes)"

[Topic 2] 15:30 PM — 학원/버스 대기 (Transit & Gate)

공통 상황: 셔틀이 오는데 도도가 친구와 놀고 있음
• 👩 엄마표: T: "The yellow shuttle arrives at the bus stop." / R: If clock shows 3:35, Dodo walks to the bus stop. / IP: "Where are you at 3:35? (Bus stop)"
• 👨 아빠표: T: "Shuttle time." / R: Stop play and stand at the curb, Dodo. / IP: "Are you at the curb? (Yes)"
• 🧔 삼촌표: T: "3:35 on watch." / R: Wave goodbye to friends, walk to the yellow bus. / IP: "Did you wave? (Yes)"
• 👵 할머니표: T: "The big yellow bus is stopping." / R: Dodo, wait behind the red sign. / IP: "Are you behind the sign? (Yes)"
• 👩‍🏫 선생님표: T: "Dismissal protocol active." / R: Proceed to designated boarding zone. / IP: "Is zone verified? (Yes)"

[Topic 3] 19:30 PM — 저녁 과제 & 디바이스 (Homework & Lock)

공통 상황: 레드 폴더 과제가 비어있음
• 👩 엄마표: T: "The red folder is open." / R: 1. Write three sentences, Dodo. 2. Close folder. / IP: "Are three sentences done? (Yes)"
• 👨 아빠표: T: "No games before work." / R: Complete the red folder first. / IP: "Is folder done? (Yes)"
• 🧔 삼촌표: T: "3 sentences = 10 min game time." / R: Write 3 lines, show Dad/Mom, get timer. / IP: "Did you write 3 lines? (Yes)"
• 👵 할머니표: T: "Bedtime comes soon." / R: Put the red folder on the desk, Dodo. / IP: "Is the desk tidy? (Yes)"
• 👩‍🏫 선생님표: T: "Assignment requirement: 3 sentences." / R: 1. Complete draft. 2. Insert into red folder. / IP: "Is task submitted? (Yes)"
